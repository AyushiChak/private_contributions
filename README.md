# Contributions to private projects:

## AccessPoint

https://github.com/CS465UIDesign/AD5_2

An interactive map of the Urbana-Champaign campus that displays accessible entrances and lecture halls, elevators, water stations, and bathrooms in three buildings: Wohlers Hall, the Art & Design Building and the Armory. It allows users to report accessibility concerns within the map to the ADA Coordinator.

## Lost at Sea

https://github.com/abbyobrad/lostatsea

A first-person puzzle RPG video game, where you, the ship's captain, wake up stranded on your vessel abandoned by your mutinous crew and must figure out how to escape to land by solving a series of puzzles.

Note: The repositories linked above are kept private out of respect for team consent.
